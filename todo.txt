# AUTOMOBILE GARAGE MANAGEMENT ERP
## Complete Automobile Workshop & Service Center Management Software
### Laravel + PHP + MySQL | Production-Ready Enterprise Application

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior Laravel software architect, automobile workshop management consultant, database architect, automotive service operations expert, financial systems developer, and UI/UX designer.

Your task is to develop a complete, professional, production-ready Automobile Garage Management ERP using Laravel and MySQL.

The application must manage the complete lifecycle of an automobile workshop, from customer registration and vehicle booking to vehicle inspection, job card creation, repair estimation, spare parts allocation, mechanic assignment, service execution, quality inspection, billing, payment collection, and customer follow-up.

This must be a fully functional business management application, not a static dashboard, demo UI, or collection of disconnected CRUD pages.

The system should offer functionality comparable to professional automotive dealer management systems and modern workshop management platforms.

## 1.1 Target Market

Primary market: India.

Target businesses:

- Multi-brand automobile garages.
- Independent car workshops.
- Two-wheeler service centers.
- Four-wheeler service centers.
- Authorized automobile dealerships.
- Commercial vehicle workshops.
- Truck and bus repair workshops.
- Bike repair shops.
- Automobile detailing centers.
- Car washing centers.
- Tire and wheel alignment centers.
- Automobile electrical workshops.
- AC repair workshops.
- Denting and painting workshops.
- Engine repair workshops.
- Fleet maintenance workshops.

The architecture must support different types of vehicles and workshop operations through configurable settings.

# 29. IMPORTANT BUSINESS LOGIC AND VALIDATIONS

These are mandatory rules.

## 29.1 Customer and Vehicle Integrity

- A vehicle must be associated with an authorized customer record.
- Vehicle registration numbers must be unique within the applicable jurisdiction and tenant scope.
- Ownership changes must preserve historical ownership.
- Service history must never be overwritten.
- Odometer readings must be recorded with their source and timestamp.
- Flag suspicious decreases in odometer readings.

## 29.2 Job Card Integrity

- Every job card must reference a valid vehicle.
- Every job card must reference a customer.
- A job card must have a unique number within its configured numbering scope.
- Chargeable work must require customer authorization.
- Additional work must have a separate approval record.
- Closed job cards must not be silently modified.
- Reopening must require permission and a recorded reason.

## 29.3 Spare Parts Integrity

- Draft estimates must not reduce stock.
- Approved parts may be reserved.
- Actual stock must be deducted when parts are issued.
- Unused parts must be returned through a stock transaction.
- Negative stock must be prevented unless explicitly configured.
- Every movement must have a source document.
- Stock adjustments must be auditable.

## 29.4 Billing Integrity

- Invoice totals must be calculated from invoice line items.
- Tax must be calculated according to configured rules.
- Payments must not exceed outstanding balances unless recorded as an advance or overpayment.
- Refunds must be traceable.
- Posted invoices must not be silently edited.
- Credit notes and reversals must preserve financial history.

## 29.5 Transaction Safety

Use database transactions for:

- Job card conversion.
- Parts reservation.
- Spare parts issue.
- Stock returns.
- Invoice posting.
- Payment allocation.
- Refunds.
- Insurance settlements.

Use appropriate row locking and idempotency protection to prevent duplicate transactions and concurrency errors.
